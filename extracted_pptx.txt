Modern interviews evaluate technical knowledge, communication, confidence, and behavior.
Traditional tools rely on static questions with generic and delayed feedback, lacking real-time adaptability.
They fail to simulate dynamic, real-world interview conditions, reducing preparation effectiveness.
PrepSmartly is an AI-based multimodal interview simulation platform designed to overcome these limitations.
It integrates speech analysis, facial recognition, and live coding evaluation for realistic assessment.
A Dual-LLM architecture enables adaptive questioning and real-time evaluation.
Provides real-time micro-feedback and detailed post-interview analysis covering technical, communication, and non-verbal aspects.

INTRODUCTION
Interviews are a critical step for jobs, higher education, and career advancement.
They assess both technical knowledge and communication skills.
Many candidates underperform due to nervousness, lack of confidence, and poor response structuring.
Existing platforms rely on static Q&A formats and fail to simulate real interview scenarios.
They ignore speech tone, facial expressions, and live coding abilities, leading to incomplete evaluation.
PrepSmartly addresses these gaps by providing a realistic, adaptive, AI-driven interview simulation environment.

CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK

Interdisciplinary Insights and Practices for Sustainable Growth (I3PSG 2026)
‹#›
02-04-2026
PrepSmartly overcomes the limitations of traditional interview preparation by providing a realistic and adaptive AI-driven simulation system. It combines real-time feedback, dual LLM evaluation, and multimodal analysis using technologies like MediaPipe and Deepgram to assess candidates across technical, communication, and behavioral aspects, while ensuring data privacy through a secure architecture.
In the future, the system can be enhanced with more advanced AI models for better question generation and evaluation, along with improved multimodal analysis such as posture and gesture tracking. It can also include personalized learning recommendations, multilingual support, job portal integration, and immersive technologies like Virtual Reality to create a more effective and realistic interview experience.
